# Qayem visual identity

Product: Qayem / قيّم. Independent interview and employee evaluation software.

Ink/wine #3b2030 anchors the navigation and headings. Wine #7c3654 identifies primary actions; bronze #a56332 provides restrained emphasis. White and neutral gray surfaces keep dense evaluation tables readable. Green, amber and red retain their semantic meanings for evaluation outcomes only.

The product mark is an original SVG check inside a geometric tile, with bilingual wordmarks. IBM Plex Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Sans remain the locale fonts under their respective third-party licenses. There is no organizational seal or affiliation artwork.

Tokens: frontend/src/assets/styles/design-system.css. Shared patterns: product.css. Maintain RTL logical spacing, visible focus, labeled forms, responsive navigation, reduced motion, meaningful empty/error states and scrollable tables. DemoNotice explains read-only or loopback practice mode and links to the in-product guide.
